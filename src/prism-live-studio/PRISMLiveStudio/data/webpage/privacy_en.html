In the process of signing up for and using PRISM Live Studio, personal information is collected and used pursuant to Article 15, Paragraph 1, Subparagraph 4 of the Personal Information Protection Act (Execution and Performance of Contracts). The personal information collected and used is as follows:


Items of Personal Information Collected:

<When signing up>

• When signing up via social media account: Nickname, SNS ID, email address, profile image

• When signing up via email address: Email address (ID), name, password

• When using NAVER Cloud B2B services: Nickname, member identifier (internal identifier randomly granted to individual members), profile image


<When paying for subscription products>

• Payment details (information of the product subscribed, date and time of payment, payment receipt information), payment STATUS information, email address


<When making customer inquiries>

• Email address


<When participating in events>

• Information required for prize delivery (delivery address, mobile phone number)


For further details, please refer to the Privacy Policy.
